Welcome

Verbum Domini, Latin for “the Word of the Lord”, is a free guide to the Catholic faith in plain English. It is for the curious, for anyone on the way into the Church, and for Catholics who want to know their faith better. Everything here comes from Scripture, the Catechism and the Church’s own documents, and every page names its sources.
