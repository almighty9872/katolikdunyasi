Records to gather

  1. Parish registration and OCIA form

    The parish’s own form with contact details, background and the names of the family. Each parish has its own.

    Everyone

  2. Birth certificate or photo ID

    The parish records the full name and the date and place of birth in its registers.

    Everyone

    Canon 877 §1

  3. Baptismal certificate from the church of baptism

    A certificate or signed letter from the church that baptized, giving the date and the rite. For an Orthodox Baptism, chrismation is presumed even if the certificate does not mention it.

    Baptized in another Christian church

    Canon 876 · Ecumenical Directory, n. 99

  4. Recently issued Catholic baptismal certificate

    Ask the parish of baptism for a new copy. The baptismal register carries notes of Confirmation, marriage and other changes, and a recent copy shows them. Many parishes ask for one issued in the last six months; that is practice, not law.

    Baptized Catholic

    Canon 535 §2 · Canon 895

  5. Marriage certificate

    The civil certificate, and the church certificate if there was a church wedding. The parish checks whether the Church recognizes the marriage before reception.

    Married now (civil or church wedding)

    Canon 1117 · Marriage Navigator

  6. Records of every earlier marriage

    For each earlier marriage of either spouse: the marriage certificate and the final divorce decree. The tribunal uses them to examine the earlier marriage.

    Either spouse was married before, and that spouse is alive

    Canon 1085 · Canon 1671 · Marriage Navigator

  7. Death certificate of the previous spouse

    A death certificate frees the surviving spouse. Without one, the bishop must declare the death presumed after an inquiry.

    A previous spouse has died

    Canon 1707

  8. Sponsor’s letter from their parish

    Many parishes ask the sponsor’s own parish to confirm that the sponsor meets the conditions. This is practice, not law.

    The sponsor belongs to another parish

    Canon 874 §1 · Sponsor Check

  9. Consent of a parent or guardian

    A signed consent from at least one parent or guardian, who also takes part in the preparation.

    Under 18

    Canon 98 §2 · Canon 868 §1 · Children and Teens

  10. Sworn statements of witnesses

    When no certificate can be had, the declaration of one reliable witness proves a Baptism, and so does the sworn word of someone baptized as an adult.

    Records were lost or cannot be obtained

    Canon 876
