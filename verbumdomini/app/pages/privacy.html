Privacy Policy

We collect nothing unless you write to us

In short

This site collects nothing about you unless you write to us. There are no analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no ads, no social media buttons and no accounts. The only outside service is Cloudflare Turnstile, a spam check on the Contact page.

What your browser keeps

Your browser keeps a few things (in its localStorage) so the site stays the way you left it:

  • your settings: theme, text size, accessibility options, keyboard shortcuts and the sidebar
  • your Bible reading tools: text size, font, spacing, page width, background and which extras are shown
  • the pages you opened, so search can list them as recently viewed
  • your progress: chapters read, where you stopped in each book, highlights, bookmarks and notes, the part of the Catechism you last read and your place in today’s Rosary
  • Bible books you opened, so you can read them offline, and the whole Bible if you save it for offline reading
  • on an iPhone, a count of your visits, so Safari shows the “Add to Home Screen” note once, on your third visit, and whether you closed it

None of this is sent to us. We can’t see it. Clear your browser’s data for this site and it’s gone.

Backup

You can save your progress and settings to a file and load it again later. Your browser makes the file and it stays on your device. Nothing is stored on our server.

Search

Search runs in your browser. What you type is not sent anywhere.

Listening

“Listen” uses the voices built into your browser and device. Some browsers also offer online voices, such as Microsoft’s in Edge or Google’s in Chrome. If you pick one, your browser sends the Bible text being read to that company to turn it into speech. It sends nothing about you. If you’d rather it didn’t, choose a voice that works offline.

“Keep the screen on” only stops your screen from dimming while a chapter is open. Sharing a verse uses your device’s own share menu.

Links to other sites

Some links go to other sites, such as BibleGateway, Vatican News or vatican.va. Nothing loads from them until you click, and once you’re there, their privacy policies apply.

Hosting

Cloudflare hosts the site. Like any web host, it may keep standard server logs for security and performance. We don’t have access to them. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

All fonts come from this site, not from Google Fonts or any other service.

The contact form

When you send a message from the Contact page, your name (if you give one), your email address and your message reach us by email through Cloudflare. We keep them only in our inbox, and we use them only to reply to you. We don’t share them, add you to a mailing list or market to you.

Turnstile checks that you’re not a bot. It runs only on the Contact page and doesn’t track you for advertising. See Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy statement.

Changes

If anything here changes, we’ll update this page. Questions? Write to us through the Contact page.
