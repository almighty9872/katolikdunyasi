Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect nothing unless you write to us

The short version

Unless you write to us, this site collects nothing about you. No analytics, no tracking, no cookies, no ad networks, no social media buttons, no accounts. The only third-party tool is Cloudflare Turnstile, and it runs only on the Contact page to keep spam out of the form (more below).

What your browser remembers

A few things are saved in your own browser (localStorage) so the site behaves the way you left it: light or dark theme, whether the sidebar is shown and which of its sections you closed, the pages you have opened (to list recently viewed pages in search), the boxes you folded away, the section of the Catechism you last read (for the Welcome page’s “Continue” card), and your reading tools for the Bible (text size, font, spacing, page width, background, and which extras are shown).

None of this is ever sent to a server. We can’t see it. Clear your browser data and it’s gone.

On your first visit the theme follows your device’s light or dark setting. Once you press the sun or moon button, your choice is kept.

Search

Searching the site and the Bible happens in your browser. The index and the Bible text are files from this site; what you type is not sent anywhere.

Your progress

Your progress (the Bible chapters you have read, where you stopped in each book, your highlights, bookmarks and notes on verses, the section of the Catechism you last read and your place in today’s Rosary) is kept in your browser like the settings above. So are the Bible books you have opened, so they can be read without a connection, and the whole Bible if you choose to save it for offline reading. You can save your progress and settings to a file and restore them yourself: the file is made in your browser and stays on your device. Nothing about your progress is stored on our server.

Listening to a chapter

“Listen” reads a chapter aloud with the voices of your own browser and device; the site sends nothing. Some browsers offer online voices (for example Microsoft’s natural voices in Edge, or Google’s in Chrome): with those, your browser sends the text being read to that company to turn it into speech. The words are those of the Bible chapter, nothing about you, but if you prefer, pick a voice that works offline.

“Keep the screen on” only asks your device not to dim the screen while a chapter is open. Sharing a verse uses your device’s own share menu.

Links to other sites

Some links open other sites, such as BibleGateway for another translation of a passage, Vatican News for the thought of the day, or vatican.va for Church documents. Nothing is loaded from them until you click, and once there, their own privacy policies apply.

Hosting and server logs

The site runs on Cloudflare, which keeps copies of the pages close to you so they load fast. Like any web server, Cloudflare may keep standard access logs for security and performance. Those logs belong to Cloudflare, not to us, and we have no access to them. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for details.

Fonts

All fonts are served from this site. No requests go to Google Fonts or any other outside service.

The contact form

When you use the form on the Contact page, your name (if you give one), your email address and your message come to us by email through Cloudflare (Workers and Email Routing). Your address is set as the reply-to, so we can answer you. Nothing is stored on the site or in a database, only in our inbox. We use it to reply to you and for nothing else: no sharing, no mailing lists, no marketing.

Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you’re not a bot. It runs only on the Contact page and doesn’t track you for advertising; see Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy statement.

Changes

We may update this page from time to time. Questions? Use the form on the Contact page.
