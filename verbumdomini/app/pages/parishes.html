Free resources, no account needed

  • A daily readings widget for parish websites: the liturgical day and its colour, the Mass readings of the Canadian lectionary and the Rosary mysteries of the day, in light, dark or compact form.
  • Print-ready handouts: the Order of the Mass, a Confession card, a Becoming Catholic (OCIA) bulletin insert and a guide to praying the Rosary.
  • Sunday bulletin texts with a copy button: becoming Catholic, preparing for Confession, Mass posture for visitors, Advent and Lent.
  • Office checklists for accessible bulletins under the AODA, CRA charitable receipts, and intake for Baptism, First Reconciliation and Communion, Confirmation and Marriage.

The parish directory

About 250 parishes of the Archdiocese of Toronto, searchable by area, Mass day and time, language and Confession times. Each parish has its own page with its address, map links, phone, website, bulletin and notices. Listings start from the Archdiocese’s public directory and are marked once the parish keeps its own.

For parish staff

Staff with an @archtoronto.org address, or an address at the parish’s own website domain, claim their parish with a six-digit code sent by email. The dashboard then lets them edit Mass, Confession and Adoration times, link the weekly bulletin and livestream, post notices that expire on their own, print a pew card with QR codes, and add other editors.
