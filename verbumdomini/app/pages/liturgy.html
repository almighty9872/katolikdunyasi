Liturgy

Hubert and Jan van Eyck · The Adoration of the Lamb

The liturgy is the public worship of the Church. Its heart is the Mass, where Christ offers Himself to the Father and feeds His people with His Body and Blood. These pages walk you through the Mass, explain how a parish should celebrate it and why, and guide you through Confession.

The Holy MassThe six parts of the Mass, with what the priest says and how the people answer.
Celebrating the MassWho does what at Mass, when to stand, sit and kneel, and why.
ConfessionHow to go to confession, step by step, with an examination of conscience.

Where to start

  • Going to Mass for the first time? Read The Holy Mass. It gives the words of the Mass and tells you when to stand, sit and kneel.
  • Want to know why things are done the way they are? Read Celebrating the Mass. It is also a guide for readers, servers, musicians and other ministers.
  • Looking for the exact rule? Each section of Celebrating the Mass gives the paragraph numbers of the General Instruction of the Roman Missal, the Church’s official rules for the Mass.
  • Preparing for Confession? The Confession guide takes you through it step by step.
