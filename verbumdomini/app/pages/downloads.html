The Bible, offline

The whole Douay-Rheims Bible, with Bishop Challoner’s notes, can be saved on your device so that every book opens without a connection. Open the Holy Bible and choose Download in the bar at the top. It saves the text inside the site, not as a separate file, and you can update it from the same button.

Your progress and settings

The site keeps your reading on this device: chapters read, your place in each book, highlights, bookmarks and notes, your place in the Catechism and the Rosary, and your settings. You can save all of it to a file and load it on another device or browser.

Good to knowThe same two buttons are in the bar at the top of every page, as small icons on the right. Loading a file combines its reading progress with what is already on this device, and replaces your settings with the ones in the file.
