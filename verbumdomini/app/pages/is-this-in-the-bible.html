Is This in the Bible?

Common challenges to Catholic teaching, answered from Scripture

Each answer below quotes the Douay-Rheims, the classic Catholic Bible in English. Click any reference to read the verse in context. The Church reads Scripture together with Tradition and the teaching of the apostles’ successors, and the verses here show where each teaching is rooted in the written Word.

The Church and Its Authority

Where does the Bible say the Bible alone is the rule of faith?

Scripture never lists its own books and never says that only written texts bind Christians. Saint Paul told the Thessalonians to keep what they had received in speech as well as in his letters.

Therefore, brethren, stand fast; and hold the traditions which you have learned, whether by word, or by our epistle.

2 Thessalonians 2:14

He called the Church the pillar and ground of the truth, and Saint Peter warned that Paul’s letters were being twisted by readers who had no one to guide them.

But if I tarry long, that you may know how you ought to behave yourself in the house of God, which is the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth.

1 Timothy 3:15

See also John 21:25 and 2 Peter 3:16.

Where is the Pope in the Bible?

Jesus changed Simon’s name to Peter, which means rock, and gave him alone the keys of the kingdom.

And I say to you: That you are Peter; and upon this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it. And I will give to you the keys of the kingdom of heaven. And whatever you shall bind upon earth, it shall be bound also in heaven: and whatever you shall loose on earth, it shall be loosed also in heaven.

Matthew 16:18-19

In the ancient kingdom of Judah the king’s steward carried the keys of the house and ruled in the king’s name, and the office passed to a successor (Isaiah 22:22). After the Resurrection Christ gave Peter the care of the whole flock.

When therefore they had dined, Jesus says to Simon Peter: Simon, son of John, love you me more than these? He says to him: Yea, Lord, you know that I love you. He says to him: Feed my lambs. He says to him again: Simon, son of John, love you me? He says to him: Yea, Lord, you know that I love you. He says to him: Feed my lambs. He said to him the third time: Simon, son of John, love you me? Peter was grieved, because he had said to him the third time: Love you me? And he said to him: Lord, you know all things: you know that I love you. He said to him: Feed my sheep.

John 21:15-17

See also Luke 22:32, where Jesus prayed that Peter’s faith would not fail so that he could strengthen the others.

Where does the Bible give the Church authority to decide doctrine?

When a dispute arose over circumcision, the apostles and elders met in Jerusalem and issued a binding decision in the name of the Holy Spirit.

For it has seemed good to the Holy Spirit and to us, to lay no further burden upon you than these necessary things:

Acts 15:28

Christ gave the power to bind and loose to Peter and then to the apostles together, and told His followers to bring unresolved disputes to the Church.

And if he will not hear them: tell the church. And if he will not hear the church, let him be to you as the heathen and tax collector. Amen I say to you, whatever you shall bind upon earth, shall be bound also in heaven; and whatever you shall loose upon earth, shall be loosed also in heaven.

Matthew 18:17-18

Where does the Bible say the apostles had successors?

After Judas died, the apostles chose Matthias to take his office, quoting the Psalms.

For it is written in the book of Psalms: Let their habitation become desolate, and let there be none to dwell in it. And his bishopric let another take.

Acts 1:20

Paul later told Timothy to hand on what he had received to faithful men who would teach others in turn (2 Timothy 2:2), and told Titus to appoint priests in every city (Titus 1:5).

The Sacraments

Where does the Bible say the Eucharist is the Body and Blood of Christ?

In the synagogue at Capernaum Jesus said it plainly, and when His listeners objected He repeated it more strongly.

If any man eat of this bread, he shall live for ever; and the bread that I will give, is my flesh, for the life of the world. The Jews therefore strove among themselves, saying: How can this man give us his flesh to eat? Then Jesus said to them: Amen, amen I say to you: Except you eat the flesh of the Son of man, and drink his blood, you shall not have life in you. He that eats my flesh, and drinks my blood, has everlasting life: and I will raise him up in the last day. For my flesh is food indeed: and my blood is drink indeed.

John 6:52-56

Many disciples left Him over this teaching, and He let them go (John 6:67). Saint Paul wrote that receiving the Eucharist unworthily is a sin against the Body and Blood of the Lord, which would make no sense if the bread were only a symbol.

Therefore whoever shall eat this bread, or drink the chalice of the Lord unworthily, shall be guilty of the body and of the blood of the Lord. But let a man prove himself: and so let him eat of that bread, and drink of the chalice. For he that eats and drinks unworthily, eats and drinks judgment to himself, not discerning the body of the Lord.

1 Corinthians 11:27-29

Where does the Bible say to confess sins to a priest?

On the evening of the Resurrection Jesus gave the apostles the power to forgive sins or to withhold forgiveness. They could only use that power if they heard what the sins were.

He said therefore to them again: Peace be to you. As the Father has sent me, I also send you. When he had said this, he breathed on them; and he said to them: Receive you the Holy Spirit. Whose sins you shall forgive, they are forgiven them; and whose sins you shall retain, they are retained.

John 20:21-23

Saint James connected the priests of the Church with the forgiveness of sins and told believers to confess them.

Is any man sick among you? Let him bring in the priests of the church, and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord. And the prayer of faith shall save the sick man: and the Lord shall raise him up: and if he be in sins, they shall be forgiven him. Confess therefore your sins one to another: and pray one for another, that you may be saved. For the continual prayer of a just man avails much.

James 5:14-16

Where does the Bible allow babies to be baptized?

Peter’s first sermon promised the gift of baptism to his listeners’ children.

But Peter said to them: Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ, for the remission of your sins: and you shall receive the gift of the Holy Spirit. For the promise is to you, and to your children, and to all that are far off, whomever the Lord our God shall call.

Acts 2:38-39

The apostles baptized whole households, which in the ancient world included children and servants (Acts 16:15; Acts 16:33; 1 Corinthians 1:16). Paul compared baptism to circumcision, which Israel gave to boys on the eighth day.

In whom also you are circumcised with circumcision not made by hand, in despoiling of the body of the flesh, but in the circumcision of Christ: Buried with him in baptism, in whom also you are risen again by the faith of the operation of God, who has raised him up from the dead.

Colossians 2:11-12

Where does the Bible say baptism saves?

Saint Peter wrote it in those words.

Whereunto baptism being of the like form, now saves you also: not the putting away of the filth of the flesh, but the examination of a good conscience towards God by the resurrection of Jesus Christ.

1 Peter 3:21

Jesus told Nicodemus that no one enters the kingdom without being born of water and the Spirit, and Ananias told Paul to be baptized and wash away his sins.

Jesus answered: Amen, amen I say to you, unless a man be born again of water and the Holy Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God.

John 3:5

And now why do you tarry? Rise up, and be baptized, and wash away your sins, invoking his name.

Acts 22:16

Where is the anointing of the sick in the Bible?

The sacrament is described in James 5:14-15, quoted above: the priests of the Church pray over the sick person and anoint him with oil, and his sins are forgiven. The apostles anointed the sick in the same way during Jesus’ ministry (Mark 6:13).

Where does the Bible say marriage cannot be dissolved?

Jesus set aside the divorce allowed under Moses and returned marriage to what it was at creation.

What therefore God has joined together, let no man put asunder. And in the house again his disciples asked him concerning the same thing. And he says to them: Whoever shall put away his wife and marry another, commits adultery against her. And if the wife shall put away her husband, and be married to another, she commits adultery.

Mark 10:9-12

Paul repeated the command as a command of the Lord (1 Corinthians 7:10-11).

Where does the Bible support unmarried priests?

Jesus spoke of those who give up marriage for the sake of the kingdom.

For there are eunuchs, who were born so from their mother’s womb: and there are eunuchs, who were made so by men: and there are eunuchs, who have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven. He that can take, let him take it.

Matthew 19:12

Paul, who was unmarried, recommended the same for those who could accept it, because it leaves a person free for the Lord’s work.

But I would have you to be without anxiety. He that is without a wife, is concerned for the things that belong to the Lord, how he may please God. But he that is with a wife, is concerned for the things of the world, how he may please his wife: and he is divided.

1 Corinthians 7:32-33

Celibacy for priests is a discipline of the Latin Church, which the Church could change. Eastern Catholic Churches ordain married men, and the Latin Church has received married former Protestant ministers as priests.

Faith, Works and the Life to Come

Where does the Bible say we are saved by faith alone?

The only verse in Scripture that uses the words “faith only” denies it.

Do you see that by works a man is justified; and not by faith only?

James 2:24

The Church teaches that salvation is a free gift of grace that no one can earn, and that real faith shows itself in love and obedience. Paul described the faith that counts as faith working through charity, and taught that God will judge each person by his works.

For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision avails anything, nor uncircumcision: but faith that works by charity.

Galatians 5:6

Who will render to every man according to his works. To them indeed, who according to patience in good work, seek glory and honour and incorruption, eternal life:

Romans 2:6-7

See also Philippians 2:12 and Matthew 25:31-46.

Where is purgatory in the Bible?

Judas Maccabeus had sacrifices offered for soldiers who had died in sin, and the book praises him for it. Prayer for the dead only makes sense if the dead can still be helped.

And making a gathering, he sent twelve thousand drachms of silver to Jerusalem for sacrifice to be offered for the sins of the dead, thinking well and religiously concerning the resurrection, (For if he had not hoped that they that were slain should rise again, it would have seemed superfluous and vain to pray for the dead,) And because he considered that they who had fallen asleep with godliness, had great grace laid up for them. It is therefore a holy and wholesome thought to pray for the dead, that they may be loosed from sins.

2 Maccabees 12:43-46

Paul described a fire on the day of judgment that tests each person’s work, in which a man can suffer loss and still be saved.

Every man’s work shall be manifest; for the day of the Lord shall declare it, because it shall be revealed in fire; and the fire shall try every man’s work, of what sort it is. If any man’s work abide, which he has built after that, he shall receive a reward. If any man’s work burn, he shall suffer loss; but he himself shall be saved, yet so as by fire.

1 Corinthians 3:13-15

Jesus spoke of a sin that will not be forgiven “in the world to come”, which implies that some sins are (Matthew 12:32). Paul prayed for his friend Onesiphorus, who had apparently died, that he would find mercy “in that day” (2 Timothy 1:16-18).

Why do Catholic Bibles have more books?

The Catholic Old Testament includes seven books (Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch and 1 and 2 Maccabees) and parts of Esther and Daniel that Protestant Bibles left out in the sixteenth century. They were part of the Greek Old Testament used by the apostles and most early Christians, and the councils of Rome (382), Hippo (393) and Carthage (397) listed them with the rest of Scripture. The Feast of the Dedication that Jesus attended in John 10:22 is the feast whose origin is told in 1 Maccabees 4:56-59.

Mary, the Saints and Images

Where does the Bible say to honor Mary?

The angel greeted her with honor, and Elizabeth, filled with the Holy Spirit, called her blessed and the mother of her Lord.

And the angel being come in, said to her: Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with thee: blessed art thou among women.

Luke 1:28

And she cried out with a loud voice, and said: Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb. And from where is this to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

Luke 1:42-43

Mary herself said that every generation would call her blessed. Catholics do that when they pray the Hail Mary, whose first lines come from these verses.

Because he has regarded the humility of his handmaid; for behold from henceforth all generations shall call me blessed.

Luke 1:48

From the cross Jesus gave His mother to the beloved disciple as his own mother.

When Jesus therefore had seen his mother and the disciple standing whom he loved, he says to his mother: Woman, behold your son. After that, he says to the disciple: Behold your mother. And from that hour, the disciple took her to his own.

John 19:26-27

Where does the Bible say to ask the saints to pray for us?

Christians already ask one another to pray for them (James 5:16). Those who have died in Christ are still alive and still part of the same family.

For he is not the God of the dead, but of the living: for all live to him.

Luke 20:38

In heaven the elders present the prayers of God’s people before the Lamb.

And when he had opened the book, the four living creatures, and the four and twenty elders fell down before the Lamb, having every one of them harps, and golden vials full of odours, which are the prayers of saints:

Revelation 5:8

The letter to the Hebrews calls the faithful departed a great cloud of witnesses around us (Hebrews 12:1).

Does the Bible forbid statues?

The commandment forbids making images to worship as gods (Exodus 20:4-5). The same God ordered Moses to make golden cherubim for the Ark of the Covenant and a bronze serpent that the people were to look at to be healed.

You shall make also two cherubims of beaten gold, on the two sides of the oracle. Let one cherub be on the one side, and the other on the other. Let them cover both sides of the propitiatory, spreading their wings, and covering the oracle, and let them look one towards the other, their faces being turned towards the propitiatory with which the ark is to be covered.

Exodus 25:18-20

And the Lord said to him: Make a brazen serpent, and set it up for a sign: whosoever being struck shall look on it, shall live. Moses therefore made a brazen serpent, and set it up for a sign: which when they that were bitten looked upon, they were healed.

Numbers 21:8-9

Catholics honor statues and images for the person they show. Worship belongs to God alone.

Doesn’t the Bible say to call no man father?

In Matthew 23:9 Jesus warned against pride and against putting any teacher in the place of God. The apostles did not read it as a ban on the word. Stephen addressed the Jewish elders as fathers (Acts 7:2), John wrote to the fathers of the Church (1 John 2:13), and Paul called himself the father of the Corinthians.

For if you have ten thousand instructors in Christ, yet not many fathers. For in Christ Jesus, by the gospel, I have begotten you.

1 Corinthians 4:15

Where does the Bible say the Mass is a sacrifice?

Malachi foretold a pure offering made to God in every place among the nations, something the Temple in Jerusalem could never provide.

For from the rising of the sun even to the going down, my name is great among the Gentiles, and in every place there is sacrifice, and there is offered to my name a clean oblation: for my name is great among the Gentiles, says the Lord of hosts.

Malachi 1:11

At the Last Supper Jesus spoke of His body “given” and His blood “shed” for the apostles, the language of sacrifice (Luke 22:19-20), and Paul set the Christian altar alongside the altars of Israel and of the pagans (1 Corinthians 10:16-21). The Mass makes the one sacrifice of the cross present on the altar.
