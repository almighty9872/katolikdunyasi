OCIA Companion Tools

Practical help for parish teams, sponsors and adults joining the Catholic Church

The Order of Christian Initiation of Adults (OCIA) guides unbaptized adults, baptized Christians from other traditions and unconfirmed Catholics into full communion with the Catholic Church. These companion tools help parish teams, sponsors and inquirers verify baptismal validity, check sponsor eligibility, map liturgical rites, organize required records, navigate youth initiation, resolve marriage questions and check scriptural references.
