OCIA

Practical answers for adults preparing to join the Catholic Church

OCIA, the Order of Christian Initiation of Adults, prepares adults for Baptism, Confirmation and first Communion, usually received at the Easter Vigil. Questions about marriage, documents and sponsors come up early in the year, so each topic here has its own page with the steps, an example and the canon law behind it. The stages of OCIA itself are explained on How to Become Catholic.
