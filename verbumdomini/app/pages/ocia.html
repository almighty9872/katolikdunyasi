OCIA

Practical answers for adults preparing to join the Catholic Church

OCIA, the Order of Christian Initiation of Adults, prepares adults for Baptism, Confirmation and first Communion, usually received at the Easter Vigil. Questions about marriage come up early in the year, and the Marriage Navigator answers them one step at a time, with the canon law behind each answer. The stages of OCIA itself are explained on How to Become Catholic.
