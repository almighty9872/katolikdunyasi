Newsletter

The Verbum Domini newsletter is an occasional email about major additions to the site, such as new guides, books and translations, and other news worth sharing. It is free, and it comes only when there is something to tell.

Subscribe to the newsletterEnter your email address to sign up.

How it works

After you subscribe, beehiiv, the service that sends the newsletter, emails you a confirmation link. The subscription starts when you click it. If the email has not arrived after a few minutes, look in your spam or promotions folder.

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Your address is used only to send the newsletter and is never shared; the Privacy Policy gives the details.

Other ways to follow

New articles appear on the blog, which can also be followed in a feed reader. Corrections and suggestions for the site go through the Contact page.
