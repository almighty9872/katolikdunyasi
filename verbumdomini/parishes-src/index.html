Free for everyone, no account needed

  • An embeddable daily card with the liturgical day, season colour, the Mass readings of the Canadian lectionary and the Rosary mysteries of the day, in light, dark or compact form.
  • Print-ready PDFs: the Order of the Mass, a Confession card, a Becoming Catholic (OCIA) insert and a Rosary guide.
  • The Print Generator: bulletin covers with the Sunday readings, pew cards, bookmarks, saint handouts, welcome brochures and wedding and funeral Scripture packets, with any parish’s times and a QR code, printed or saved as PDF.
  • Sunday bulletin texts on OCIA, Confession, Mass posture for visitors, and Advent and Lent, in plain English, ready to paste.
  • Checklists for accessible bulletins under the AODA, CRA charitable receipting, and intake for Baptism, First Reconciliation and Communion, Confirmation and Marriage.

For verified parish staff

Once a parish is claimed, its staff can sign in with a code sent to their email and:

ToolWhat it does
DashboardThe liturgical day, reminders for the coming holy days, how complete the parish’s listing is, and monthly visit counts (no visitor data).
Schedules and holy daysSunday and weekday Masses, Confession and Adoration, plus special dates: Christmas, Holy Week, holy days and holidays, suggested for the year ahead.
Web bulletin and noticesAn accessible weekly bulletin on the parish page (pastor’s message, Mass intentions, announcements), the PDF bulletin link and notices that expire on their own.
Clergy, office and ministriesPastor and clergy, office hours, accessibility features, languages and the parish’s ministries and groups.
Print and PDF studioBulletin inserts, pew cards, bookmarks and brochures with the parish’s times and QR code, and the pew QR poster.
Quick reference and widgetsCanon Law and Catechism numbers with plain summaries and links to vatican.va, and website widgets for the readings and the parish’s live schedule.
Staff editorsThe person who claimed the parish can add or remove other editors.

Claims are open to parish staff with an @archtoronto.org address or an address at the parish’s own website domain. Every change is logged with the name of the editor who made it.

About this portal

It is part of Verbum Domini, a free Catholic library and guide in English. Parish listings start from the Archdiocese of Toronto’s public directory; corrections from parishes replace them. Questions and corrections go through the contact form.
